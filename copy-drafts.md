## RAW MATERIAL — for future blog / content drafts

From Musa's voice notes, not yet shaped into copy. Useful seeds:

- Pre-Saudi: taught alphabet + reading at home, then Qaeda Noraniyah / Qaeda Baghdadiyah, then once they could read they could pick up school books and translate words themselves
- Used Arabic cartoons to support immersion
- Once at school in Saudi, they got many hours of immersion per day — more hours = better
- The flexibility insight: most Madrasah/Tahfeedh classes are after school and conflict with sports / other activities; kids end up disliking Madrasah; "it doesn't have to be one or the other"
- The split-slot insight: 1-to-1 means a family can split a 1-hour slot across siblings, e.g. 3 children at 20 minutes each
- Tips-for-parents seed: keep Quran playing in the background, Arabic cartoons, more listening exposure, helping children read before expecting deep understanding
- Future content idea: cartoon-style Sahaba stories with dialogue (NOT in AI-generated style — stylised cartoon)
